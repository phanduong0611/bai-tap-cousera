Nghệ An

Nghe An is the largest province in Vietnam, famous for being the birthplace of President Ho Chi Minh, Cua Lo beach, and a tradition of academic excellence.

I. Cua Lo Beach & VinWonders CuaHoi
  • The most famous beach in Nghe An, with fairly clean water and delicious seafood.

  • You can combine this with a trip to Song Ngu Island and VinWonders amusement park:

  • Suitable for summer trips, groups of friends or families.

II. Kim Lien relic (Làng Sen quê Bác)
  • This is the birthplace of Ho Chi Minh.

  • A peaceful place, rich in historical and cultural value.

  • Suitable for exploring Vietnamese history.

III. Khe Kem waterfall – Vườn quốc gia Pù Mát
  • One of the most beautiful places in western Nghe An province.

  • It features waterfalls, primeval forests, and very cool air.

  • Ideal for backpacking, trekking, and weekend picnics.
